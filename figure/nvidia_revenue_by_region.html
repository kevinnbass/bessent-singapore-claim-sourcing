Nvidia revenue by region, on both bases

Quarterly revenue in $ billions. Until its report for the quarter ended July 27, 2025 Nvidia counted revenue by the customer's billing address. From the next report it counts by the customer's headquarters, and the Singapore line is gone. Numbered markers show when the US rules on its chip sales to China changed.

Policy changes marked on the charts

US government actions on which Nvidia data-centre chips may be sold to China. A marker shows when a rule changed, not why a line moved. Each point is a whole quarter drawn at its last day, so the point just after a marker still includes sales made before the change.

    Not marked: instructions reported to have come from China's government (press accounts; no published rule), additions to the Entity List, and rules on other subjects. After the last quarter shown, on November 16, 2026, Commerce filed a rule that requires a licence for advanced chips going to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil [22].

    Hollow markers are quarters Nvidia did not report on their own. Fourth quarters are the full-year figure less the nine-month figure; the quarter ended July 31, 2022 is the nine-month figure less the quarters before and after it.

    China includes Hong Kong. Other is every remaining country; on the headquarters basis it includes customers headquartered in Singapore.

    The composite joins two different measures. For the quarter ended July 27, 2025 the United States is 23.5 by billing address and 32.9 by headquarters, so the rise across the break is partly the change of basis. The dashed lines in panel C carry the headquarters figures back over the four earlier quarters Nvidia restated, so both measures can be read where they overlap.

    All three panels share one date axis and one vertical scale, May 2022 to October 2026 and 0 to 80, so a quarter and a dollar figure sit at the same place in each.

    Both tables count Nvidia's direct customers. Neither shows where goods were shipped.
